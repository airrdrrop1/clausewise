FREELANCE DESIGN SERVICES AGREEMENT (SAMPLE — fictional)

This Agreement is made between BrightNest Studio Ltd. ("Client") and
A. Freelancer ("Designer"), effective 1 March 2026.

1. Services. Designer will create a logo suite and brand guideline document
   ("Deliverables") as described in Exhibit A.

2. Fees and Payment. The total fee is USD 2,400. Client shall pay all
   invoices within sixty (60) days of receipt. Client may deduct a 5%
   "administration charge" from any invoice at its sole discretion.

3. Revisions. Designer will provide unlimited revisions until Client is
   fully satisfied, at no additional charge.

4. Intellectual Property. All work product, including preliminary drafts
   and rejected concepts, becomes the exclusive property of Client upon
   creation, whether or not payment has been made.

5. Term and Termination. This Agreement runs for twelve (12) months and
   renews automatically for successive twelve-month periods unless either
   party gives notice ninety (90) days before renewal. Client may
   terminate this Agreement at any time, for any reason, with immediate
   effect and without any termination fee. Designer may terminate only
   for material breach, with sixty (60) days' written notice.

6. Confidentiality. Designer shall keep all Client materials confidential
   for five (5) years after termination.

7. Liability. Designer's total liability under this Agreement shall be
   unlimited. Client's total liability is limited to the fees paid.

8. Delivery. Designer will deliver the final files by 15 April 2026.
   Time is of the essence.
